## Getting a result

1. Open the **Scan** tab.
2. Choose **Open Camera** to take a photo, or **Import from Library** to choose one with the
   system photo picker.
3. For the most useful guidance, use a clear, full-body photo with one visible person, even
   front-facing light, and a simple background.

AuraFit’s score and tips are subjective styling and photography guidance about the outfit and
photo. They are not a measurement of a person, health, attractiveness, identity, or worth.

## Camera and Photos permissions

- AuraFit requests **Camera** access only after you choose **Open Camera**. If it is off, the
  **Camera Access Needed** message offers **Open Settings** to change the iPhone setting or
  **Use Library** to continue without Camera access.
- **Import from Library** uses Apple’s system photo picker. AuraFit receives only the image you
  select; it does not ask for broad Photo Library read access.
- AuraFit requests **Photo Library (add)** access only when you tap **Save** or enable
  **Auto-save exports to Photos** in Settings. You can decline it and still use AuraFit.

## Saved fits and exports

Completed scans are saved locally in **History** on the iPhone. You can favorite or delete an
individual fit from its History context menu. To remove every saved fit and its images, open
**Settings** → **Data** → **Delete All Fits**, then choose **Delete Everything**. This cannot be
undone. Deleting the app also removes its local app data.

**Share Scorecard** opens the iPhone share sheet. **Save** writes an export to Photos only after
you choose that action and grant permission. Any shared item is handled by the destination you
select.

## Purchases and restore

AuraFit Pro subscriptions and one-time template unlocks are handled by Apple through StoreKit.
To restore eligible purchases, open **Settings** → **Membership** → **Restore Purchases**. If
the app cannot contact the App Store, check your connection and try again. AuraFit does not
receive your payment details.

## What to include in a support request

Do not send source photos, Apple IDs, receipts, payment details, or other sensitive data. For a
technical issue, include the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, the exact steps,
expected and actual result, and whether it repeats. Screenshots or recordings are optional and
should not include private photos unless you deliberately choose to share them through the
owner-approved support route.
