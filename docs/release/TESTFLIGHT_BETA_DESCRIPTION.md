# TestFlight Beta App Description

AuraFit is a private, on-device outfit and photo coach for iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
Take or import a full-body photo to receive subjective styling and photo-quality guidance.
Completed results stay in local History and can be shared as a scorecard in any of three
styles or as a short reveal clip. Everything in the app is free; there is nothing to buy.

This beta asks for feedback on camera/import guidance, analysis clarity, accessibility,
local persistence, and exports. AuraFit has no account and does not upload selected photos
for analysis. Results are subjective creative guidance, not a measurement of attractiveness,
health, or personal worth.
